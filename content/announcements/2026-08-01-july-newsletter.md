We're looking at Ruby Retreat AU, RubyConf, and Kansai RubyKaigi in the rearview mirror, and looking forward to RubyConf Africa, Ruby for Good, the Ruby Taiwan track at COSCUP, and two XO Ruby series events in August. XO Ruby Vancouver and Seattle are both happening, but sadly XO Ruby Salt Lake City has been cancelled. Brighton Ruby and RubyConf Austria videos have all been published! We had one of our busiest months ever for contributions with 123 PRs merged from 25 contributors, partially due to a Hack Day at RubyConf where we welcomed 8 new contributors, and 13 contributions!

## Open CFPs

🎤 [RubyWorld Conference](/events/rubyworld-2026/cfp)(December 03-04, 2026 in Matsue, Japan) - [CFP](https://2026.rubyworld-conf.org/en/news/2026/07/call-for-proposals/) closes August 14.
🎤 [RubyConf India](/events/rubyconf-india-2026/cfp)(November 21, 2026 in Hyderabad, India) - [CFP](https://cfp.rubyconf.in/) closes August 15.

> None of these feel quite right? Check out our list of [meetup cfps](https://www.rubyevents.org/cfp?kind=meetup)! Many are open year round and always looking for new talks.

## July's events

📆 [Ruby Retreat](/events/ruby-retreat-au-2026)(July 03-06 in Sydney, Australia) - A long-weekend retreat in a lodge on the Sydney Harbor.
📆 [RubyConf](/events/rubyconf-2026)(July 14-16 in Las Vegas, NV) - the 25th edition of the world's first Ruby conference.
📆 [Kansai RubyKaigi 09](/events/kansai-rubykaigi-09)(July 18 in Otsu, Japan) - A day-long regional conference for the Kansai community.

> Did you attend a Ruby event this month that isn't on the list? Let us know with an [issue](https://github.com/rubyevents/rubyevents/issues/new) or [PR](https://github.com/rubyevents/rubyevents/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)!

## August's events

📆 [Ruby Taiwan Track @ COSCUP](/events/coscup-2026)(August 08-09 in Taipei, Taiwan) - A track dedicated to Ruby at COSCUP, a large cross-technology conference. 🎤 Check out the 11 scheduled [talks](/events/coscup-2026/talks)! 🎟️ [Tickets](https://coscup-tw.kktix.cc/events/preregist) appear to be free, but registration is limited.

📆 [XO Ruby Seattle](/events/xoruby-seattle-2026)(August 08 in Seattle, WA) - The XO Ruby series is known for great speaker lineups in small, interesting spaces that are worth the drive. 🎤 Check out 5 scheduled [talks](/events/xoruby-seattle-2026/talks) from KJ Loving, KOW, Jed Schneider, Ryan Davis, and Gary Tou! 🎟️ [Tickets](https://buy.stripe.com/9B6bJ24HJe9q3Q9g2A43S0m) are available for $100 USD.

📆 [XO Ruby Vancouver](/events/xoruby-vancouver-2026)(August 15 in Vancouver, Canada) - The XO Ruby series is also well-known for being a road trip series of events - everything from above applies to this event as well, especially regarding the [speaker lineup](/events/xoruby-vancouver-2026/talks) which includes Kyle d'Oliveira, Fernando Perales, and Joé Dupuis! 🎟️ [Tickets](https://buy.stripe.com/3cIeVe1vxd5m4Ud03C43S0n) are available for $100 USD.

📆 [RubyConf Africa](/events/rubyconf-africa-2026)(August 21-22, 2026 in Nairobi, Kenya) - The largest Ruby conference in Africa that's been running since 2015. 🎤 Check out the 15 scheduled [talks](/events/rubyconf-africa-2026/talks)! 🎟️ [Tickets](https://rubyconf.africa/tickets) are available for KSH 1,500, or KSH 3,500 with the networking party.

📆 [Ruby for Good](/events/ruby-for-good-2026)(August 27-30 in Sharpsburg, MD) is a long weekend retreat dedicated to building open-source projects for nonprofits. While the projects are ongoing, this event serves to handle larger initiatives and start new projects. 🎟️ [Tickets](https://ti.to/codeforgood/rubyforgood) include food and accommodations and are available for $450.

_If you were planning on attending [XO Ruby Salt Lake City](/events/xoruby-salt-lake-city-2026), please be advised that [it has been cancelled.](https://www.xoruby.com/blog/so-whats-up-with-xo-ruby-2026/)_

> Prepare more than a month ahead by checking out our [calendar](https://www.rubyevents.org/events/2026) or the full [list of events](https://www.rubyevents.org/events).

## Contributions

This month, we merged [123 PRs from 25 contributors!](https://github.com/rubyevents/rubyevents/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+merged%3A2026-07-01..2026-07-31) 77 PRs were [new event data and videos](https://github.com/rubyevents/rubyevents/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+merged%3A2026-07-01..2026-07-31+label%3Acontent). We held a Hack Day at RubyConf where we welcomed 13 contributions from 8 new contributors to the project! ❤️
