## Contributions

This month, we merged [131 PRs from 22 contributors!](https://github.com/rubyevents/rubyevents/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+merged%3A2026-10-01..2026-10-31) 87 PRs were [new event data and videos](https://github.com/rubyevents/rubyevents/pulls?q=is%3Apr+is%3Amerged+merged%3A2026-10-01..2026-10-31+label%3Adata).

### Big Updates

Meetups have been totally refactored! There's now one event per instance of a meetup, making our data more granular, and our data model more consistent. You can now specify which instances of a meetup you attended and we can list out individual events. As part of the process, meetups got a UI refresh.

If you want an example, [find one in your area](https://www.rubyevents.org/events/meetups), or take a look at [WNB.rb's meetup](https://www.rubyevents.org/events/series/wnb-rb) for a fully specified example of an online meetup.

This is a major update for meetups, but there might be some growing pains as we figure out where it makes sense to display individual meetup instances throughout the site and on the calendar. Please reach out and open an issue if you encounter any problems or have suggestions.
